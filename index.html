Privacy Policy for Sudi Tenga Calculator

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Sudi Tenga Calculator is a calculator app created to help members of the Chakma community calculate interest and repayment amounts using the traditional monthly flat simple-interest Sudi Tenga method. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter calculation details such as a principal amount, monthly interest rate and loan duration. The app uses these details to calculate monthly interest, total interest and the total repayment amount.

According to the app's current design, calculation details and calculation history are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to a server operated by the app developer. The developer does not receive or maintain a copy of your calculation history.

2. Local Storage and Deletion

Calculation history stored on your device remains under your control through your device and the app. You can remove locally stored app data using the app's available controls, where provided, or your device settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data may delete locally stored history.

3. Information Collection, Analytics and Advertising

The app does not use advertising services, analytics services or crash-reporting services. The app does not require you to create an account and, in its current design, does not collect or send your calculation entries or history to the developer.

4. App Updates and Third-Party Services

The app may use Expo Application Services (EAS Update) to check for and download app updates. When this feature is enabled, the app connects to Expo's update service. The update service may receive technical information needed to identify the app and determine which compatible update to provide, such as app version, platform and runtime/update information. This update process is separate from your calculator entries and locally stored calculation history; the app does not intentionally send those calculation details as part of its calculation feature.

Expo's privacy information is available at https://expo.dev/privacy.

5. Sharing of Information

The app developer does not sell or share your calculation entries or locally stored calculation history. Technical requests needed to deliver OTA updates are handled by Expo as described above. No advertising or analytics partners are integrated into the app according to its current configuration.

6. Data Security

Because calculation details and history are stored locally, you should protect access to your device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and the developer cannot guarantee the security of information stored on your device.

7. Children's Privacy

The app does not ask users to provide personal information or create an account. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features, services or data practices change. Any revised policy will be posted at this same URL with an updated effective date. Please review it periodically.
